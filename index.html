Straight answers.

Do I have to change how I write estimates?
No. CCC ONE keeps working exactly as it does now. RepairReply reads the EMS file it saves.
Will customers get texts for jobs I already approved or cancelled?
No. The sequence stops when a job is booked, cancelled, or when the customer replies. A text that's gone stale is dropped, never sent late.
Whose number do the texts come from?
Yours. A local number on your shop's name. Replies come to your inbox.
Can I keep sending texts myself?
Yes. Leave auto-send off and every text waits for you to tap Send.
What about CCC Secure Share?
RepairReply runs on the EMS export today. A Secure Share connection is on the roadmap.
What happens to my customer data?
It stays your shop's data. Twilio carries the texts. Google sees appointments only if you connect your calendar.

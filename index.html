Messages

Where it fits

It sits next to CCC ONE. It doesn't replace anything.

What RepairReply does

  • Reads the estimates you already write. From the EMS export CCC ONE can already produce, on the desktop or cccone.com.
  • Chases the ones that went quiet. Timed texts to customers who have not booked, in your words.
  • Gives you an inbox you can answer. On your phone, with the estimate attached.
  • Shows what it earned. Hourly, from your own repair data.

What it leaves alone

  • Your estimating. Same CCC ONE, same workflow, same screens.
  • Your DRP and insurer connections. Nothing touches the files you share with insurers.
  • Repair status updates. If you use CCC's status texting, keep it. RepairReply is about the estimates that never became repairs.
  • Your data. It stays in your own database, on your own server or ours. Only Twilio and Google ever see a message, under your accounts.

Questions shops ask

Straight answers.

Do I have to change how I write estimates?

No. CCC ONE keeps working exactly as it does now. RepairReply reads the EMS file it already saves. If you are on cccone.com, the same export exists there.

Will customers get texts for jobs I already approved or cancelled?

No. The sequence stops when a job is booked, cancelled, or when the customer replies. A text that is more than two days overdue is dropped, never sent late.

Whose number do the texts come from?

Yours. We register a local number to your shop's name through Twilio. Replies to that number come to your inbox and, if you want, forward to your cell.

Can I keep sending texts myself?

Yes. Leave auto-send off and every text waits for you to tap Send. Many shops run it that way for the first few weeks, then switch it on.

What about CCC Secure Share?

EMS export works today on both CCC ONE desktop and cccone.com. A Secure Share connection is on the roadmap so shops can switch as CCC rolls it out, with no change to RepairReply on your side.

Where does my customer data live?

In a database that belongs to your shop. Nothing is pooled across shops, and nothing is sold. Only Twilio (to send texts) and Google (if you connect your calendar) ever see a message, through your own accounts.
